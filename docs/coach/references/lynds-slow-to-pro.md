## Start procedure (technique 5, in order)

1. Shift up to 3rd before the gate on a 450 (2nd -> 3rd if you have quick-shift).
2. Find your lean point: front wheel light, not flipping. Setup-dependent (swingarm length, fork height, sag) — practice in your own local world and line the bottom of the screen up with a fixed part of the bike (seat end, bottom of the bars, front of the bars) as a repeatable reference.
3. Hold the push-forward button into the gate. Acts like a holeshot device: compresses the front, stops the wheelie, and the bike is already moving the instant the gate drops even with the clutch in.
4. Gate drops: dump the clutch, release push-forward, and do a fast stand-up-sit-down immediately (not a held stand). Small gain, but decisive with 35 riders into turn one.
5. First half of the start straight decides it: if the holeshot isn't there, check up and tuck underneath on the inside rather than getting sent to the moon in the turn-one carnage.
