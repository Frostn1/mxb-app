Features

Everything your MX Bikes garage needs

From finding a track to swapping engine sounds — managed from one window.

One-click install

Add to Library and MXB App downloads the mod, extracts it, and drops the files straight into the right mods folder — tracks, bikes or rider.

Browse the catalog

Search and preview the full mxb-mods.com catalog — listings, images and galleries — without leaving the app.

Library management

See everything you've installed at a glance. Multi-select to uninstall, move, or organize many mods into folders at once.

Locker: models & sounds

Swap each bike's model or engine sound in and out with a click. Park variants and switch whenever you like.

Editable presets

Save combinations of gear and settings as presets, then rename or re-tune any slot later — no need to rebuild from scratch.

3D rider preview

See your rider and gear rendered in 3D right inside the app, so you know exactly how a setup looks before you ride.

Multi-host downloads

MediaFire, Google Drive (including Drive folders) and MEGA links are resolved automatically, so you rarely touch a browser again.

Safe by design

Uninstalls go to the Recycle Bin and applying a preset keeps a byte-for-byte backup — nothing is destroyed behind your back.

Guided onboarding

A first-run tour walks you through every screen, and a help hint on each one explains what it does — so you're never lost.

FAQ

Good to know

Which mods are supported?

Tracks, bikes and rider gear are all supported — search, install, and manage them end to end. Bike model and sound swaps are handled in the Locker. More mod types are planned.

Which download hosts work?

MediaFire, Google Drive (including Drive folders) and MEGA are resolved automatically, and direct links are downloaded as-is. The one exception is a MEGA folder link — open the mod page to grab that one manually.

What archive types can it extract?

.zip and .7z are extracted natively, and packaged .pkz files are placed as-is. .rar isn't supported yet.

Do I need Windows?

To install mods into a real game, yes — MX Bikes is Windows-only. A macOS (Apple Silicon) build exists mainly for working on the app itself.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. MXB App is free and MIT-licensed. The source lives on GitHub, where you can also file issues and follow releases.

Is it affiliated with MX Bikes or mxb-mods?

No. MXB App is an independent community tool. MX Bikes and mxb-mods.com are the property of their respective owners.
